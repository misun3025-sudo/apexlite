National Museum of Korea — "Gaya Spirit: Iron and Tune"

Year
Dec 2019 – Mar 2020
Location
National Museum of Korea, Seoul
Category
Exhibition & Media Art

Project contextContext

"Gaya Spirit: Iron and Tune" ran at the National Museum of Korea from December 2019 to March 2020, pairing mounted warrior figures with a curved media façade.

Our scopeScope

  • Exhibition lighting
  • Timecode-based lighting/media system

DeliveryDelivery

Brightness was controlled separately so the form and texture of the physical figures and sculptures in front of the curved media façade could be read, and the system ran for about three months without a resident operator.

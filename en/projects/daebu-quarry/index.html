Daebu Mine Media Façade, Ansan

Year
2022
Location
Daebu Mine (former quarry), Ansan
Category
Exhibition & Media Art

Scope of workScope

  • Lighting design
  • On-site operation

Event programmeProgramme

An outdoor media façade project combining projection mapping on a former quarry cliff with LED costume performance.

Site conditions & design approachConditions & Approach

On natural terrain with no existing structure, power, cabling and rigging positions were planned from scratch, with the projection surface and performer lighting controlled separately.

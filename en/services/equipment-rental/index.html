How to rent

Two ways to hire

A

Dry hire

  • Equipment only, without design.
  • Send dates, location and your fixture list (or show scale) and we'll reply with a setup and quote.
  • Full inventory list on request.
B

With programming & operation

  • grandMA3 and MA2 operators on site.
  • Available together with lighting design and pre-programming.
  • Operation only is possible when a design already exists.

Lighting Design
Concepts, plots and fixture lists built from the creative brief and the venue. For film, we design to camera exposure and frame, not the naked eye.
Lighting Programming
Pre-programmed grandMA3 / MA2 show files, LTC/MTC timecode sync with video, audio and show control. Laser control available.
Technical Advance
Venue drawings, rigging, power and load-in conditions checked and turned into a workable plan for your schedule.
Fixture & System Review
Your fixture list matched against available inventory, with substitutions that preserve the look.
Technical Drawing
Lighting plots, patch sheets, DMX universe maps, magic sheets, cue sheets, rigging points and load data.
Equipment & Crew Coordination
Owned equipment plus a trusted local network of technicians.
On-site Lighting Direction
Focus, rehearsal cueing and show operation, or show file handoff and direction of local operators.
Venue Lighting System
System design for venues, exhibitions and permanent installations, including unattended playback.

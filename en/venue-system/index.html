Venue System

Lighting systems designed for the space and how it will be run.

From venue openings and reopenings to permanent exhibitions and media spaces — lighting and control systems reviewed for their purpose and finished into something that can actually be run.

Scope

What we cover

01

Opening, reopening & renewal

Lighting system specification and layout reviewed during venue openings, reopenings and renewals.

02

System review

The purpose of the space, how it will be run, drawings, power and rigging conditions.

03

Equipment layout

Fixtures, circuits, cabling, network, DMX universes and control structure.

04

Control & programming

Consoles, media servers, show controllers and timecode linked, with show files and integration tests in the real operating environment.

05

Operation & handover

Operating manuals, backup data and staff training, plus support for technical issues after opening.

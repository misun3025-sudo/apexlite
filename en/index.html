In Korea

Bringing a show, shoot or event to Korea? We review your rider and plot, source matching fixtures and crew locally, adapt the design to the venue and support you through rehearsal and show.

Touring Abroad

Taking a Korean production overseas? We review local inventory, prepare technical riders and pre-programmed show files, and hand off documentation your local crew can work from on day one.

Technical Advance

Solve it before load-in.

A new venue means different fixtures, rigging and power. We check your rider against local inventory, propose substitutions that keep the design intact, and deliver an updated plot, patch and show file before you arrive.

  • Fixture substitution
